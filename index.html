Good questions

A few things you might be wondering.

Will repair improve my card's grade?

No grading outcome is guaranteed. Treatments such as pressing, wax/polish, recolouring, scratch removal and added substances may cause a grader to classify a card as altered. We discuss these risks before consent.

Can you repair edge whitening or holo scratches?

Missing printed pigment or foil cannot be restored to original factory condition through conservative care. We will not promise an invisible repair or replace material to simulate a factory surface.

Why is the quote based on current card value?

Structural work on rare, fragile or high-value cards carries greater handling risk, documentation needs and time. Simple surface care is priced separately without this multiplier.

How does my client portal work?

The clinic registers your card and links it to your email address. Once your private account is provisioned, sign in with that email and password to view the tracking record, quote and photographs. Clients cannot submit new cases on the website.

Can I approve a quotation in the portal?

Yes. Each card requires a signed Consent & Authorisation form confirming the exact quotation and treatment scope. Clinic staff must verify the agreement before treatment starts.

Can you treat every type of damage?

No. Severe water damage, mould, torn cardstock, deep foil loss and structurally compromised vintage cards may be too risky. We may recommend preservation without physical intervention.
